This paper investigates whether parameter-efficient modules (PEFTs) like LoRA can be composed via arithmetic operations to generalize to unseen task combinations. The authors demonstrate that arithmetic operations on PEFT parameters create effective models for combined tasks without additional training, and analyze the conditions under which composition succeeds, offering a novel approach to multi-task generalization.
