This work identifies a universal linear direction within the internal representations of large language models that reliably distinguishes truthful from hallucinated responses. The authors find this "truthfulness hyperplane" emerges consistently across model sizes and domains, and demonstrate it can be used for both analyzing and intervening on LLM truthfulness. The paper also provides insights into the mechanistic nature of how LLMs encode truthfulness information.
