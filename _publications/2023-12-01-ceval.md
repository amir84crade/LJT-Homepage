C-Eval is the first comprehensive Chinese evaluation suite for large language models, consisting of 52 subjects across math, physics, chemistry, humanities, and more. The benchmark spans four difficulty levels (high school, college, professional, and advanced) and has become a standard evaluation for Chinese-language foundation models. The paper also provides a leaderboard and analysis of model capabilities across domains.
