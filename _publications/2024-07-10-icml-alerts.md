This paper introduces "in-context sharpness" as a signal derived from internal LLM representations that reliably predicts hallucinated outputs. The authors show this sharpness measure correlates strongly with whether a model's response is factually grounded, and develop a mitigation framework that uses this signal to reduce hallucinations at generation time, without requiring additional training or retrieval.
