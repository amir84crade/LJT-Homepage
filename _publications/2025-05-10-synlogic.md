This work introduces SynLogic, a framework for synthesizing large-scale verifiable reasoning data to improve LLM logical reasoning capabilities through supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning. The synthesized data comes with ground-truth verification signals, enabling more reliable training signals than previous approaches. The method demonstrates strong performance across multiple reasoning benchmarks.
