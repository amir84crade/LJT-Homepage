This work identifies a key perception bottleneck in vision-language models (VLMs) when performing chart understanding tasks. By analyzing model behavior through targeted interventions and probing methods, the authors reveal how visual encoding limitations impede VLM performance on chart comprehension, and propose solutions to mitigate this bottleneck. The paper also releases the Vision4Chart benchmark.
